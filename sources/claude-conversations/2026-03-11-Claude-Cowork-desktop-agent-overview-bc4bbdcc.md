**Claude Cowork** (often just called "Cowork") is Anthropic's desktop AI agent product, launched in January 2026. Here's a quick overview:

**What it is:** Cowork brings Claude Code's agentic capabilities to the desktop app for non-technical work. You give Claude access to your local files, set a task, and it gets to work — delivering completed outputs directly to your file system.

**How it differs from regular Claude chat:** Instead of asking Claude a question and manually carrying out the steps yourself, you give it access to the files or apps involved in the task and let it handle the work. In short, regular Claude tells you *how* to do something — Cowork actually *does it*.

**Key capabilities include:**
- Direct local file access (read and write without manual uploads), sub-agent coordination for complex tasks, professional outputs like Excel spreadsheets and PowerPoint presentations, and long-running tasks without context limits.

**Privacy & safety:** Cowork runs locally in an isolated virtual machine on your computer. You choose which folders and connectors Claude can see, and it can't access anything without your explicit permission. Cowork conversation history is stored locally on your device, not on Anthropic's servers.

**Availability:** It's available as a research preview for paid plans (Pro, Max, Team, Enterprise) on macOS and Windows, accessible through the Claude desktop app.

**Connectors:** Organizations can connect Cowork to tools like Google Drive, Gmail, DocuSign, and FactSet, and deploy customizable plugins across domains like financial analysis, engineering, and human resources.

It's essentially Claude evolving from a chatbot into a true digital coworker that can autonomously complete multi-step tasks on your behalf.
