## The Big Idea

Imagine you're the charge nurse on a busy floor. You have 30 patients. You could try to handle every single patient yourself — vitals, meds, charting, call lights, everything. You'd be one really overworked person trying to do it all.

**Or** you could have a team: an RN for every 4 patients, a tech, a unit secretary, a pharmacist checking orders. Each person has a specific job and they all talk to each other.

rUv's theory says AI works the same way. Instead of building one giant super-brain AI (like one nurse trying to do everything), you're better off building a **team of smaller AI "agents"** that each have a specialty and coordinate with each other, like a well-run unit.

## So Where Does "Time" Come In?

Here's where it gets interesting. Think about how time feels different depending on what you're doing:

When you're doing a rapid response, 5 minutes feels like 30 seconds. Your brain is processing *faster* than normal. When you're waiting for a doctor to call back at 3am, 5 minutes feels like an hour.

rUv built a system where each little AI agent can **experience time at its own speed**. One agent working on a hard problem can slow down and "think deeply" (like you during that rapid response), while another agent handling something routine zips through quickly. They're all running on the same clock, but each one gets to use that time differently based on what it needs.
