## Claude

The Apple AirTag uses a **CR2032** coin cell battery. It's a standard, widely available 3V lithium battery — the same type found in many watches and small electronics. Apple designed the AirTag so you can easily replace it yourself by pressing down and twisting the back cover.

Battery life is roughly one year with everyday use, though that can vary depending on how often the AirTag is pinged or plays sounds.

## Claude

Those are **CR2016** batteries — wrong size for an AirTag. They look similar but the CR2016 is thinner (1.6mm vs 3.2mm).

You need a **CR2032**. Same diameter, just twice as thick. Put those back and look for the 2032 on the same rack — they're usually right next to each other.
