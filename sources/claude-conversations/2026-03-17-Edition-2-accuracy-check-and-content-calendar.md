## Claude

These look clean. The design language is sharp. "The Signal" branding, bold type, minimal layout. That's a strong visual identity for Instagram.

For LinkedIn though, I'd pump the brakes on adding visuals to every post. Here's why:

The Distribution Architecture's platform evidence section (lines 148-153) flags that LinkedIn's algorithm tends to penalize posts with external links, and multiple practitioner reports from 2025-2026 suggest native text posts outperform image posts for reach on LinkedIn. The whole Loud Hawk LinkedIn strategy is built around text-first, standalone observations. That's where the authority lives.

The risk with adding visuals to LinkedIn text posts is that the image becomes the main event and the text becomes a caption. For a brand built on sharp written analysis, that's a dilution, not an upgrade.

Here's how I'd think about it:

**LinkedIn:** Keep text-only as the default. The writing is the product. If you ever attach a visual, it should be a data chart or something that genuinely adds information the text can't convey alone. Not a branded card restating the headline.

**Instagram:** This is exactly where these assets belong. News pills, The Signal cards, motion versions. Instagram is the visual intelligence packaging channel. Go hard here. The motion assets especially could be a differentiator since most aviation accounts post static everything.

So the split stays clean: LinkedIn is the text authority channel. Instagram is the visual intelligence channel. The same observation can live on both platforms, but the format is native to each surface. That's already in the guardrails (lines 654-658: don't cross-post formats).

Are the motion assets ready to deploy, or still in development? And for today, let's get your Tuesday LinkedIn text post built. What Gap observation do you want to run with?
