__STEP 1. Tell Us About Yourself__ We encourage you to complete all sections to provide a comprehensive applicant profile and to expedite processing. The questions we'll ask you will help us to better understand which type of applicant you are.

__STEP 2. Select an Academic Program__ Choose your area of study ranging from certificate and non-degree seeking programs, to associate, bachelor's and master's degrees. Review the Embry-Riddle Worldwide __academic catalog__ online for more information about degrees and admissions criteria.

__STEP 3. Complete Your Educational History__ Be prepared to list any colleges or universities you've attended. You'll be asked to submit required official transcripts electronically or by mail. Official transcripts must be sent directly from the issuing institution to Embry-Riddle at the address below.

Embry-Riddle Aeronautical University Worldwide Imaging Department 1 Aerospace Boulevard Daytona Beach, FL 32114

Embry-Riddle also accepts electronic transcripts from approved online transcript ordering services. Please utilize the following email address when initiating transcript requests: __wwimage@erau.edu__

__STEP 4. Pay the Application Fee__ Once you’ve filled out all required fields on the application, your application will reflect 99% complete. Take the final step towards submitting your application by clicking on ‘Submit your Application’ where you’ll be directed to the final steps of verifying and successfully submitting your application. Once complete, you'll be directed to make payment for the $50 non-refundable application fee, unless you selected a valid application fee waiver. Your application for admission is valid for one year from the submitted date. We'll send you a 'Thank You for Submitting Your Embry-Riddle Application' email once complete!
