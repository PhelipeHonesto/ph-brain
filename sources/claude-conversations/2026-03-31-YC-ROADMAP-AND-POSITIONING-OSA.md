*Summary: **Conversation overview**

This conversation centered on building out the go-to-market strategy for OSA Connect, a sub-brand product under the master brand OSA (Orange Sunshine Aviation). The person is a working airline pilot and Embry-Riddle graduate building an AI-powered charter brokerage, with OSA Connect as the product and Loud Hawk as a companion editorial/content brand. The conversation covered four interconnected workstreams: YC application strategy, growth projections, organic channel planning, and brand positioning.

On YC strategy, the discussion established that solo founders face a higher bar but can overcome it through traction. The Fall 2026 batch was identified as the primary target, with the Winter 2027 batch as the backup. Two interactive artifacts were built: a growth projection dashboard (React/JSX) showing revenue, quote requests, operator count, and deals closed across three scenarios (12%, 18%, and 25% week-over-week growth) aligned to YC batch deadlines, and a phased organic growth roadmap covering six channels across five phases from April 23 through December 31, 2026. The final workstream was a full April Dunford positioning stress-test, blending her framework with Chris Walker's demand creation model and Dave Gerhardt's founder-led brand approach. Key decisions reached: the primary value driver is bilateral trust (not adversarial buyer-vs-operator framing), the beachhead segment is entrepreneurs and small business owners, and the moat emphasis is pilot credibility as the trust layer with AI as the capability layer. The pilot framing was split: "airline pilot" for consumer-facing contexts and "ATP-rated pilot" for industry and operator-facing contexts, with a firm rule never to name the current employer to avoid employment policy conflicts and perception issues. Financial product claims including pre-funded bookings and escrow-protected transactions were explicitly excluded from day-one positioning since that infrastructure is Phase 2. The locked company one-liner is "AI-powered charter brokerage, vetted by an airline pilot." The master brand OSA is positioned as "the intelligent infrastructure layer for private aviation transactions," with the new category frame held for when payment infrastructure is live. The person confirmed the category strategy of subcategory now, new category later.*
