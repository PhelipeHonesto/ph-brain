## Loud Hawk Instagram Strategy

**Before I go deeper: what's the handle?** I need to see your current profile, bio, grid, and post history to give you a real audit. Share it and I'll do a specific teardown.

Here's the strategy framework based on the Loud Hawk voice, the 2026 algorithm, and your constraints as a solo operator.

### The core insight

Most private aviation Instagram is lifestyle content: cabin shots, tarmac sunsets, champagne. That's the noise. Loud Hawk's edge is the same on Instagram as in the newsletter: **reveal the operational layer**. The gap between what the client sees and what the broker built. That's save-worthy, share-worthy content that no one else in the space is making.

### Content pillars (4 buckets, rotate weekly)

**Insider intel** — what clients don't know they don't know. Example carousel: "5 things your charter broker checks before quoting you." This is the Loud Hawk signature. High save rate because people reference it later.

**Ops reality** — how trips actually get built. Reel format works here: 30 seconds, face-to-camera or text overlay. "Here's what happens in the 4 hours between your call and your quote." This is your discovery engine for new followers.

**Founder / pilot** — your ATP, the ERJ-175, the São José dos Campos story, the dual citizenship angle. This humanizes the brand and builds the credibility that separates you from every other charter account. Behind-the-scenes from your actual flying life.

**Market intel** — short data points, route trends, seasonal demand patterns. Static posts or simple graphics. "Aspen charter demand is up 40% in December. Here's why that matters for pricing." Quick hits, basically.

### Format mix (what the 2026 algorithm rewards)

Carousels get slightly better engagement than Reels in 2026, but Reels drive discovery to new audiences. So the mix should be roughly 60% carousels (save + share driver), 30% Reels (reach driver), 10% Stories with polls/Q&A (reply and DM driver).

Saves are a stronger signal than likes in 2025-2026, and carousel posts with progressive value drive saves naturally. This is perfect for Loud Hawk because the newsletter content is inherently reference-worthy. Repurpose each edition into 2-3 carousels.

### Engagement seeding (your instinct is correct)

This is the highest-ROI activity for a new account. Spending 10-15 minutes a day engaging with ideal clients and industry peers through thoughtful comments, story responses, and real conversations is a two-way experience that the algorithm rewards.

**The daily routine (15 minutes):**

Build a target list of 30-40 accounts across three tiers. Tier 1: charter brokers and operators you want relationships with (these are also potential operator leads for OSA Connect). Tier 2: HNW-adjacent accounts (luxury travel, business leaders, real estate, sports management). Tier 3: aviation community (avgeek accounts, pilot accounts, FBO accounts).

Every day: comment meaningfully on 5-10 posts from your target list. Not "great post" but actual insight. Reply to 3-5 stories. Send 1-2 genuine DMs when someone posts something you have a real take on. The goal is not to pitch. The goal is to become a familiar, credible name in their feed before they ever visit your profile.

**This is where the pilot credential pays off.** When you comment on an operator's post with actual operational knowledge, you stand out from every marketing account in their mentions.
