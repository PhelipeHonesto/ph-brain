Here's what's in the document and why it's structured the way it is.

**Section 1: Content Repurposing System** — The step-by-step extraction process that turns each newsletter edition into a LinkedIn post and Instagram Story without creating separate content workstreams. Includes the specific matrix showing how each edition section (Signal, Main Piece, Quick Hits) maps to each channel, plus the content banking process for off-week material. Time budget: 3–4.5 hours per 2-week cycle.

**Section 2: LinkedIn Profile Strategy** — Full profile architecture (headline, banner, Featured section, About) designed as a landing page for Loud Hawk and passive discovery channel for OSA. Plus the operating rules: post types, frequency caps, engagement strategy, and what to avoid.

**Section 3: Instagram Strategy** — Deliberately minimal for Phase 1. Three content types (edition Story, key line Story, optional observation post). 5–10 minutes per cycle. Keeps the brand present without creating a production burden.

**Section 4: Weekly Operating Rhythm** — Locked schedule integrating all channels into publish week and off week tables with time estimates per task. Clear priority stack for what to cut when time is tight.

**Section 5: Phases 2–4 Roadmap** — Each phase has a defined purpose, specific actions, and a decision gate with named criteria and required evidence. Gate 1 (Edition 10): prove the editorial product works. Gate 2 (Edition 24): prove it generates real OSA Connect demand. Gate 3 (Month 24): prove the ecosystem connection is functioning. Phase 4 covers longer-form content, paid intelligence tier, and automation — all contingent on stage gate progress from the Execution Memo.

**Section 6: Ecosystem Connection Map** — Channel ownership table showing which entity (Loud Hawk, OSA Connect, OSA institutional) owns which channel and when each activates. The bridge logic: Loud Hawk builds attention, OSA Connect converts demand, channels never bleed into each other's territory.

**Section 7: Risks and Operating Warnings** — The five most likely failure modes, all grounded in the Execution Memo's existing warnings.

Everything is calibrated to a solo founder with 10–15 hours per week and zero budget for new tools. The gates are strict enough to prevent premature expansion.
