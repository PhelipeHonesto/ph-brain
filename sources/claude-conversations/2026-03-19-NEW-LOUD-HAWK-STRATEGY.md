## Andromeda (Ads)

Andromeda is Meta's proprietary machine learning system for ad retrieval, designed to deliver a step-function improvement in advertiser performance. It launched in late 2024 and by January 2026, it powers all Facebook and Instagram ad delivery worldwide.

**What it actually does:** Where the old system picked from thousands of ads per auction, Andromeda picks from tens of millions, fast enough to match individual users to individual ads.

**The fundamental shift:** Meta shifted away from audience-first advertising to creative-first matching. Targeting became less deterministic as interests and lookalikes no longer performed as strongly. Instead, creatives became the primary signal.

In plain terms: you no longer tell Meta who to show your ad to. Your creative tells Meta who should see it. Creative quality now accounts for 56% of all campaign performance outcomes, more than targeting, budget, placement, and timing combined.

**How Andromeda works with GEM:** Andromeda decides what can be shown (the retrieval step), while GEM determines what should be shown next (the ranking step). Think of Andromeda as choosing which ads make it onto the shelf, while GEM learns what shoppers buy and shapes what gets featured.

**What this means practically:**

- Your 2026 approach should be one campaign per objective, with budget set at the campaign level via CBO, letting the algorithm allocate spend to the best-performing ads.
- Andromeda works best with broad targeting: open age ranges, wide geographies, minimal interest-based segmentation. Targeting now happens primarily through your creative.
- If all your ads look the same, Meta will pick one and ignore the rest. Creative iteration (changing the hook) is not creative variation (changing the concept).
- Delivery cycles are faster, creative gets picked up and exhausted with remarkable speed. You need to refresh more often.

## Organic Algorithm (Feed, Reels, Stories, Explore)

There is no single algorithm. Each surface (Feed, Reels, Stories, Explore) has its own ranking system, but they share core logic.

**The four-step process for Feed:** Inventory (what's available), Signals (hundreds of thousands of data points), Predictions (how likely you are to engage), and Relevance Score (final ranking).

**Key signals in 2026:**

- Saves and shares are the most powerful signals, more valuable than likes or reactions.
- Up to 50% of your Feed is now recommended content from accounts you don't follow. This is where discovery happens.
- All videos are now classified as Reels (a mid-2025 change), and short-form video dominates discovery.
- Organic reach averages 1.65% of Page followers. Organic alone won't build the business.

**Critical platform philosophy:** Meta does not want organic content designed primarily to move users off platform. Facebook is now a revenue-generating juggernaut built on ad impressions and an algorithm designed to keep users scrolling on platform, not clicking onto other websites.

This directly impacts Loud Hawk distribution on social. Links to Beehiiv will get suppressed. The content itself needs to live on-platform (carousel posts, Reels, text posts) with the newsletter signup as a secondary action, not the primary post.
