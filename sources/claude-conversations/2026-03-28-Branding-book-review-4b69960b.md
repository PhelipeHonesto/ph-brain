# Branding book review

*Summary: **Conversation Overview**

Phelipe is the founder and CEO of Orange Sunshine Aviation (OSA), a private aviation platform he is building while working as a commercial airline pilot with an ATP certificate, Embraer E175 type rating, and Flight Instructor credentials (CFI, CFII, MEI). The company operates as a charter brokerage (OSA Connect) and aircraft advisory service (OSA Aircraft), with a media publication called Loud Hawk serving as an independent editorial intelligence channel for private aviation. The conversation was a comprehensive brand strategy and design session focused on rewriting and rebuilding the OSA brand book and website mockup.

The session began with Phelipe sharing the existing brand book and a designer brief, which Claude analyzed against prior strategic decisions. The core brand architecture decision made was a Jobs 1997-style simplification: collapsing five independent visual identities (OSA, Black Box, Tribe 7500, OSA Connect, Loud Hawk) into two systems — OSA as the master brand with sub-brands as wordmarks only, and Loud Hawk as an independently positioned editorial publication. The canonical color palette was locked to four colors: OSA Orange (#E74B1D), Near Black (#1A1A1A), Cream (#F5F0E8), and Loud Hawk Deep Purple (#432a7a). A critical decision was preserving the purple in Loud Hawk's identity rather than eliminating it — the purple hawk with orange wordmark follows the Michelin Guide model where visual independence coexists with transparent family connection via the shared orange thread. Black Box and Tribe 7500 were paused pending revenue justification.

Claude rebuilt the brand book as a complete HTML document (eight chapters covering architecture, essence, OSA visual system, Loud Hawk system, design principles, touchpoints, and deliverables), then built two website mockups — first a dark version which Phelipe rejected as too startup-like, then a cream-dominant version reflecting premium institutional positioning (Net-a-Porter, private banking aesthetic). The website copy was rewritten applying Hero's Journey (customer as hero, OSA as guide), Sparkline framework for the hero section, Monroe's Motivated Sequence for the quote preview, and UX writing principles for all CTAs (verb-first, outcome-specific). A significant copy pass removed all unbuilt technology claims — yield management as a built system, cryptographic trust layer, Trust Scores as a technical product, AI-native platform claims, and 118 innovations — replacing them with honest present-day capabilities: operator research, safety vetting, all-in pricing, and domain expertise from a working pilot. Founder credentials were corrected to: ATP Certificate, Flight Instructor (CFI, CFII, MEI), Embraer E175 Type Rating, 7+ years in aviation, dual US-Brazilian citizenship — with Embry-Riddle and SkyWest Airlines removed at Phelipe's direction. The "three options" framing was changed to "curated options" to remove an artificial constraint. Logo files were identified and embedded throughout: `lg_osa_3` confirmed as the OSA stacked logo, PERFIL_1 confirmed as the purple-background Loud Hawk avatar (primary), PERFIL_2 as white background, PERFIL_3 as orange background — with a key correction that the file labeling had been inverted in earlier passes.*
