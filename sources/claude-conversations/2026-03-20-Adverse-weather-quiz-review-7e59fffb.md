# Adverse weather quiz review

## Claude

I can see your 10.1.7 Adverse Weather quiz (Q126) and I have the full CBT material. Let me work through all 10 questions with you.

**Q1:** Flying near a thunderstorm should be avoided. Prior to entering a thunderstorm area...

**Answer: All of the above.** Per the CBT (page 33), all four actions are required prior to entering a thunderstorm area: select best altitude avoiding the freezing level, ensure all personnel seated and belted, and monitor radar constantly with 10-mile (20 preferred) clearance from intense buildups.

**Q2:** To avoid hydroplaning when landing on wet runways, use operational techniques such as minimum "safe" touchdown speed, early runway contact, early use of spoilers (if applicable), and, if possible, wheel brakes and reverse thrust to decrease aircraft landing roll.

**Answer: True.** That's essentially verbatim from the CBT (page 69).

**Q3:** To keep our flight attendant(s) safe during an unexpected encounter with moderate turbulence, the appropriate PA would be...

**Answer: "Flight attendant(s), be seated immediately; flight attendant(s), be seated immediately."** Per the Turbulence Action Table (page 8), for unexpected moderate or greater turbulence, the PA is that exact call.

**Q4:** A runway is considered contaminated when more than ____% of the runway surface area...

**Answer: 25.** Per page 50 of the CBT.

**Q5:** Flying on the downwind side and under the overhang of a thunderstorm will ensure hail avoidance.

**Answer: False.** The CBT explicitly states "Never fly under the overhang of a thunderstorm. Often this is where hail is expelled from the storm" (page 45).

**Q6:** Flight into volcanic ash MUST be avoided. To avoid flight into volcanic ash...

**Answer: "When the area and movement is not clearly defined, flights must be planned at least 100 miles from any suspected areas of volcanic ash."** Per page 64. AIRMETs don't depict volcanic ash (SIGMETs do), onboard radar can't detect it, and flying low to get below it is not a solution.

**Q7:** Low visibility taxi charts MUST be used...

**Answer: "when ATC announces that airport's SMGCS plan is effective."** Per page 48: "When weather conditions warrant, ATC announces the airport's SMGCS plan is effective. The low visibility charts must be used while the plan is in effect."

**Q8:** Takeoffs and landings are PROHIBITED on runways reporting ____.

**Answer: NIL BA.** Per page 55, a RwyCC of 0 represents NIL braking action and results in immediate closure of the runway.

**Q9:** An aircraft must not takeoff with frost, ice, or snow adhering to any critical surfaces. This is referred to as the Clean Aircraft Concept. When conditions conducive to icing exist, it is the PIC's responsibility to comply with the clean aircraft concept.

**Answer: True.** Straight from page 77.

**Q10:** We can operate our aircraft in areas of known severe icing conditions.

**Answer: False.** Per page 78: "An aircraft must not be flown into known severe icing conditions."
