# Hot Spots Q126 quiz answers

## Claude

I can see you've got the Hot Spots Q126 quiz up and the Q1 2026 CQ Hot Spots material. Let me help you knock out those 10 questions. Here are the answers based on the Hot Spots CBT:

**Q1.** Catering items (except crew water) _____ be removed from the aircraft.
**Answer: must not** (Slide 44)

**Q2.** At times, a pilots' performance and reliability may suffer as they deal with personal issues. Often, pilots are unaware that resources exist which can help them with these issues and avoid jeopardizing their standing with the Company.
**Answer: True** (Slide 41)

**Q3.** Which is NOT a mitigation strategy for hearback/readback errors?
**Answer: Only Respond to ATC During a Low Area of Vulnerability** -- The others (prioritize the communication task, use standard phraseology, cross-check readbacks, seek clarification early, manage expectation bias) are all valid mitigation strategies.

**Q4.** Overcoats must be black. Overcoats must be from an approved uniform vendor. When the overcoat is worn without epaulets, then a pilot hat must also be worn.
**Answer: True** (Slide 43, item 5)

**Q5.** During Flight Operations IEP 2025 Q4 flight deck observations, what had the highest rate of noncompliance?
**Answer: EFB Requirements** -- 24 observations, vs. Brake Release at 15. (Slide 8)

**Q6.** On the ERJ Flight Closeout, which type of thrust is the second question referring to?
**Answer: Reserve** -- That's the whole point of the Hot Spot. Pilots are misreading it as "reverse." (Slides 35-36)

**Q7.** What are the top human factors (from ASAP data) involved in hearback/readback errors?
**Answer: All of the above** (Expectation Bias, Working Memory Limits, Distraction/Multitasking, Confirmation Assumption, Similar Call Signs/Overlaps)

**Q8.** Black, soft-shell aviator jackets must be from an approved uniform vendor. In order to be identifiable as a flight deck crewmember, pilot wings and epaulets must be worn on these jackets while on duty.
**Answer: True** (Slide 43, item 6)

**Q9.** All white bucket test samples from filter vessels are to be taken under pressure. The presence of contamination is much more evident when the sample is taken from a pressurized system.
**Answer: True** (Slide 25, GMM 21.3.2)

**Q10.** Serious lacerations and concussions have occurred due to pilots walking under low clearance jet bridges.
**Answer: True** (Slide 9 references walkaround safety injuries including slips, falls, strains, cuts, and bruises)
