# Pre-seed org chart structure for sales

*Summary: **Conversation Overview**

Phelipe is the solo founder of Orange Sunshine Aviation, an AI-native charter brokerage platform he is building while working as a commercial airline pilot on reserve. This conversation covered three major areas: finalizing the pre-seed hiring plan, mapping the fundraising roadmap, and resolving the company's legal/operational infrastructure before an April 21 launch target.

On hiring, the conversation settled on a clean four-person pre-seed team: CTO (equity-heavy, builds the full agent pipeline, payment rails, Connect app, and Mission Control), Operator Relations/Forward Deployed (tech person who learns aviation, configures both schedule integrations and payment flows for each operator), Brand and Content Strategist (demand engine for SEO route pages and Loud Hawk), and a Sales Rep with base salary plus commission structured on the Paramount model (closer and account manager combined, since AI agents handle 80% of the work). A fifth hire, a Forward Deployed Engineer for complex integrations, comes later when volume demands it. Phelipe confirmed the operator relations profile should be a tech person who learns aviation, not the reverse, since he provides the domain expertise himself.

On fundraising, the conversation established the dream sequence as YC Combinator first (pre-seed, $500K for 7%) followed by a16z for the seed round. Specific partner-level fits were identified: Tom Blomfield and Harshita Arora at YC given their fintech and vertical transportation backgrounds, and Angela Strange and Alex Rampell at a16z given their direct alignment with the "every company will be a fintech company" thesis and Mercury investment respectively. The Brex parallel was discussed as a blueprint, with Henrique Dubugras and Pedro Franceschi having gone through YC before a16z backed them at a $12.3B valuation. The YC Summer 2026 batch deadline of May 4 was identified as the immediate application window, with the honest assessment that applying pre-revenue is valid and that the working simulator at 72% pass rate, ATP credentials, and validated business model are stronger than most pre-revenue applications. Aviation-focused investors were also mapped including SKY VC (formerly JetBlue Ventures) and Kenn Ricci/Directional Aviation, with a note that Ricci's Tuvoli platform is a direct competitor requiring caution. The seed round structure was confirmed as a16z leading with operator angels filling a portion of the same round at the same terms, turning the cap table into a supply-side pipeline.

On legal and operational infrastructure, the conversation resolved several key decisions. Entity structure was confirmed as Delaware C-Corp (not LLC) using Every.io for free incorporation, 83(b) election filing, EIN retrieval, and investor-ready corporate docs, with Mercury opened separately for banking. General Legal (YC W26) was identified as the solution for both the client broker agreement and operator agreement at $500 each ($1,000 total), replacing the previously estimated $3-5K attorney cost. Insurance was scoped as a four-policy stack needed from day one since the Connect app, AI agents, and Mercury payment flows all launch simultaneously: General Liability, Professional Liability/E&O, Cyber Liability, and Tech E&O/AI Liability. Corgi (YC S24) was identified for the tech-side policies, with an open question on whether aviation-specific E&O from a specialist is required, to be answered on a Thursday call with a friend who works in insurance. D&O was noted as needed before the raise, not before deal one. The all-in launch cost collapsed from an estimated $4,000 attorney fee to roughly $1,800-2,200 total for the full stack.

On brand and domain, a significant strategic decision was made: "OSA" as a standalone mark is dangerous given conflicts with an existing Spanish airport logistics company (Operador de Servicios Aeroportuarios) that holds osa.aero and uses the same acronym, particularly given plans to operate in Europe. The resolution was to treat "Orange Sunshine Aviation" as the master brand and primary public-facing identity, with Connect, Aircraft, and other services as plain-language capability descriptors rather than "OSA"-prefixed sub-brands. "OSA" becomes internal shorthand only until a trademark search clears it. A USPTO search confirmed "Orange Sunshine" is unregistered in Class 39 (transportation/aviation), making the full name legally safe globally. The domain orangesunshine.io was identified as available and the preferred operational domain, with orangesunshineaviation.com retained*
