Phelipe is asking about how backlinks change the trajectory to 200K traffic. Let me give a direct, honest answer.Backlinks are the multiplier. They're what compress that 12–24 month timeline significantly. Here's how it changes the math:

**Why backlinks matter for OSA specifically**

Google ranks new domains slowly because they have zero authority. Backlinks from trusted sites transfer authority to you, which makes your route pages rank faster and higher than they would on domain age alone. Without backlinks, you're waiting 6–12 months just for Google to trust you exist.

**The three backlink strategies that actually work**

**1. Editorial / HARO-style (highest value, slowest)**
Get quoted in aviation, business travel, or finance publications as a pilot-founder building an AI brokerage. Forbes, Business Insider, Flying Magazine, AVweb. One link from a DA 70+ site is worth 100 links from random directories. This is where your founder story and ATP credential become link bait — nobody else in the charter broker space has that angle.

**2. Programmatic / resource links (scales with route pages)**
Create genuinely useful data for each route — average charter prices, flight time, airport options, operator ratings. Travel bloggers, corporate travel managers, and aviation forums link to useful resources. Build the best Chicago→Miami private jet page on the internet and it earns links passively.

**3. Partnership links (fastest to execute)**
Every operator you work with, every FBO you mention, every aviation brand you reference — ask for a reciprocal link. Also Every.io, Mercury, General Legal — they all have founder/customer spotlights. You're using their product, pitch them a feature. Those are DA 50–70+ links you can get in weeks.

**How backlinks change the traffic timeline**

Without backlinks → 200K visits is 18–24 months minimum.

With an active backlink strategy (5–10 quality links per month) → realistically compresses to **10–14 months.** Your domain authority climbs faster, route pages rank in position 5–15 instead of page 3+, and the compounding kicks in earlier.

**The OSA-specific play**

Loud Hawk is your backlink engine disguised as a content brand. Every good piece of editorial journalism you publish is a reason for aviation media to link to you. Position Loud Hawk as the aviation industry's truth-teller and the links come to you — you don't have to chase them.

The sequence: ship route pages → build Loud Hawk editorial credibility → get quoted in aviation/business media → links flow back to both properties → domain authority rises → route pages climb → traffic compounds.

That's how you get to 200K in under a year instead of two.
