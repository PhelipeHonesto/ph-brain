IEE's pricing page lists the report fees but doesn't publish the same-day expedite fee — it shows up at checkout once you select your report type. For ERAU you'd need the **Course Report** ($195) since they want a course-by-course evaluation with GPA.

The expedite fees (Rush/Special/Same-Day) are add-ons on top of that, and they're non-refundable. Same-Day also requires all your documents in by **11 AM** the day you order.

Honestly — since Chelsea already confirmed your other vendor is accepted and admissions decisions only take 3-5 business days *after* the file is complete, paying for Same-Day probably isn't worth it unless you're rushing for a specific term start date. Do you have a deadline you're trying to hit? If not, standard 3-5 day turnaround at IEE (or whatever your current vendor is doing) gets you there fine.

To get the exact same-day price, you'd need to start an application at myiee.org/get-started — pricing shows on the order page before you pay.
