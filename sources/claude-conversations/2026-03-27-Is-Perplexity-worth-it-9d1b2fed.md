Good, I have a comprehensive picture. Let me summarize the key points about whether Perplexity is worth it.It depends on what you'd use it for. Here's the honest picture:

**The free version is solid.** For casual research and quick information lookups, the free version is excellent — most people who try it end up keeping it open alongside Google.

**Perplexity Pro ($20/month) shines for research.** For researchers, students, and knowledge workers, Pro is worth it — the academic paper access and model quality are meaningfully better than the free tier, and the time savings on research tasks pay for the subscription quickly. It distinguishes itself from other AI tools by offering real-time web search with citations and multi-model access.

**But there are some real concerns in 2026.** Perplexity has been quietly reducing what Pro subscribers get — search limits dropped from 600 to 200 per week, and Deep Research queries fell from 50 to 20 per month, all without announcements or emails to users. Some annual subscribers have even reported being downgraded mid-plan without notice.

**It's not great for everything.** For creative or technical work like writing or coding, tools like ChatGPT or Claude are better suited — Perplexity is purpose-built for research and information retrieval.

**Bottom line:** If you do a lot of research that requires cited, factual answers from current sources, Pro is a good investment. If you mostly need quick lookups, the free tier is plenty. And if your main needs are creative writing, coding, or deep analysis, you'd likely get more value from other tools.

**Where Claude holds its own (or does better):**

- **Reasoning and analysis** — once you have the information, Claude tends to do more with it. Explaining nuance, comparing perspectives, working through implications.
- **Working with your documents** — uploading a PDF or dataset and having a real conversation about it is a core strength here.
- **Coding, writing, and creative work** — areas where Perplexity openly says it's not the right tool.

**The real answer:** They're good at different things. Perplexity is a better *search engine replacement*. Claude is a better *thinking partner*. A lot of power users actually use both — Perplexity to quickly find and verify facts, Claude to analyze, write, and reason deeply about them.
