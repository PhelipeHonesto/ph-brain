Got everything I need. Here are your answers for all 10 questions:

**Q1: When the windshear caution is active, the pilot has the option of continuing the flight using the current flight director mode OR selecting windshear escape guidance by pressing the TOGA button.**
**True.** Straight from the SOPM and Systems & Equipment: with a *caution* windshear, the pilot can continue in the current FD mode or press TOGA for escape guidance. It's the *warning* windshear where the autopilot disconnects and escape guidance kicks in automatically.

**Q2: The autopilot disengages when the stick shaker is activated.**
**True.** Per Systems & Equipment: "The autopilot disengages when the stick shaker is activated." Also confirmed in the SOPM stall section.

**Q3: A ____ is an emergency or airplane system condition that requires immediate crew action.**
**Warning message (Red).** Warnings are the highest priority EICAS messages, accompanied by a triple chime and the master warning pushbutton.

**Q4: The flight director windshear escape guidance should be followed when the following indication is seen.**
Looking at that PFD image: you've got the red "WSHEAR" annunciation on the PFD and "WSHR" on the glareshield. That's a windshear *warning*, not just a caution.
**True.** When you see the red WSHEAR, you follow FD escape guidance (TOGA, MAX thrust, follow FD).

**Q5: On the Terrain Digital Display, Red dots indicate ____.**
**Terrain more than 2,000 ft above the airplane.** Per Systems & Equipment: red dots = terrain more than 2,000 ft above airplane altitude.

**Q6: The following image is associated with a ____. This means that the other airplane is approximately 20-30 seconds from the TCAS collision area. PFD shows fly-to area and VSI shows safe range.**
**Resolution Advisory (RA).** The red/green bands on the PFD and VSI with 20-30 seconds from the collision area is the RA description. TA is 35-45 seconds and shows amber, not the fly-to area/safe range indications.

**Q7: Pressing the warning or caution button acknowledges the warning or caution message and extinguishes the blinking.**
**True.** Per Systems & Equipment: pressing the master warning pushbutton acknowledges warning messages and extinguishes the blinking. Same for master caution.

**Q8: The EGPWS provides visual and aural warnings to help prevent ____.**
**CFIT (controlled flight into terrain).** Verbatim from the Systems & Equipment section on EGPWS.

**Q9: The T/O Config checks which four conditions?**
**Flaps, parking brake, pitch trim, spoilers.** Per Systems & Equipment: flap position matches MCDU setting, parking brake off, pitch trim in green range, spoilers closed. That matches the answer "Flaps, parking brake, pitch trim, spoilers."

**Q10: Based on the following image, the ____ is activated and the fly-by-wire system begins ____ when nearing the critical AOA.**
Looking at that PFD image showing the red PLIs (Pitch Limit Indicators) and the low speed area on the airspeed tape:
**Stick shaker, AOA limiting.** Per Systems & Equipment: "The stick shaker is activated and the fly-by-wire system begins AOA limiting when nearing the critical AOA."

Here are your answers for the Electrical System quiz:

**Q1: Selecting the IDG to DISC will mechanically disconnect it from the engine. This would require a mechanical writeup at the end of the flight.**
**True.** Per Systems & Equipment: selecting DISC mechanically disconnects the IDG (hold 1-3 seconds), and "a reset by maintenance is required for a mechanical disconnect of an IDG."

**Q2: Which item is NOT on the HOT Battery Bus?**
**Courtesy/Service Lights.** The Hot Battery Buses are always powered regardless of battery switch position. Items on the Hot Bus include the pressure refueling panel (fueling panel is specifically called out as being on the Hot Bus), engine fire protection, and emergency lights. Courtesy/service lights are not on the Hot Battery Bus.

**Q3: The lights inside the GPU switch illuminate to show GPU status. "AVAIL" is displayed when the AC power meets the specific quality requirements (voltage, amperage, and frequency).**
**True.** Straight from Systems & Equipment on the GPU switch.

**Q4: What are some of the sources of AC power on the aircraft?**
**All of the above.** The APU generator, GPU, and IDGs are all sources of AC power. The IDGs are the primary source (engine-driven), APU gen has the same output as an engine-driven generator, and the GPU can power the entire aircraft.

**Q5: In the case of a total loss of AC power in flight...**
**The RAT should automatically deploy and provide limited AC power.** Per Systems & Equipment: "In case of total AC power loss in flight, the RAT should automatically deploy and provide AC power for systems essential to the safe operation of the aircraft." It powers the AC Essential Bus.

**Q6: Circuit breakers can only be reset in flight as directed by the QRH or if necessary for the safety of flight. Circuit breakers are reset by maintenance personnel prior to block out.**
**True.** Per Systems & Equipment: "CBs can only be reset in flight as directed by the QRH or if necessary for the safety of flight. CBs are reset by maintenance personal prior to block out."

**Q7: The ERJ-175 has what type of circuit breakers?**
**Electronic and Thermal.** Per Systems & Equipment: "The E175 has both electronic and thermal CBs. The electronic CBs are accessed through the MCDU. Some thermal CBs can be accessed in the cockpit."

**Q8: During normal operations, when should the flight crew start the APU?**
**10 minutes prior to pushback or as necessary for passenger comfort.** Per Systems & Equipment: "Approximately 10 minutes prior to departure, or as necessary for passenger comfort, start the APU."

**Q9: Which minimum battery voltage guarantees 10 minutes of power during an electrical system failure, including two APU starts? This voltage is required for both batteries.**
**22.5V.** Per Systems & Equipment: minimum voltage for normal operation is 22.5V. Below 22.5V you reference the SOPM battery charging table, and below 21.0V requires a maintenance writeup. 22.5V is the minimum that guarantees adequate battery capacity.

**Q10: The sole purpose of the DC GPU is to assist with the _______ as needed.**
**APU start.** Per Systems & Equipment: "The sole purpose of the DC GPU is to assist with the APU start as needed."
