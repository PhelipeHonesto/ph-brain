## Claude

In typography that's called a **middle dot** (interpunct). A few ways to get it:

On Mac: **Option + Shift + 9** → **·**

So you'd type: `Challenger 350 · 4hr routing · ~960 gal`

That's the character you want — not a period, not a bullet. The middle dot sits at the visual center of the line height and reads as a clean separator.
