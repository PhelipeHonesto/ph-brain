All built. 108 cards total, pulled straight from SOPM Chapter 02 Rev 11. Here's what's in there:

**Airspeeds** (20 cards) - VMO/MMO, VFE schedule, VLO/VLE, turbulence penetration, RAT speeds

**Structural / Weights** (12 cards) - LL and LR ramp/takeoff/landing/ZFW, runway/taxiway minimums, dimensions

**Altitudes & Temps** (8 cards) - Max ops alt, T/O and landing field elevation, temp limits, single pack ops

**Engine / Powerplant** (12 cards) - ITT, starter cranking cycles (ground and flight), dry motoring, ATTCS

**Systems** (42 cards) - Battery, fuel, APU, pressurization, hydraulics, brakes, thrust reversers, autopilot, icing, EGPWS, FMS, flight controls

**Memory Items** (14 cards) - Evacuation, jammed controls, trim runaway, steering runaway, dual engine failure, battery overtemp, engine abnormal start, dual EFB failure

You can study by category or hit "All Categories" for a full random shuffle. Cards track correct/missed, and at the end you can drill just the ones you got wrong. Keyboard shortcuts work too if you're on desktop.
